# OnlySticks

> **Note:** This documentation, and the code comments throughout the repository, were written by AI (Claude, by Anthropic) at the owner's request. They describe the code as it was when they were written. If something here disagrees with the code, trust the code.

OnlySticks is an Android card-battler. You collect **stick** cards, build a deck, and fight turn-based duels against computer opponents or against a friend on the same Wi-Fi network. Your daily steps from Health Connect turn into coins and trainer progress. Your collection can be backed up to your own Google Drive.

## Features

- **Turn-based duels.** Each turn both players secretly pick a move (Jab, Lunge, Parry, Slash or Rest) within 7 seconds, and the moves are resolved together. Sticks wear down, can catch fire or get wet, and can break.
- **Collection and deck building.** Sticks come in five rarities (C, U, R, SR, UR) with random stats and hidden "tuning" traits. Decks hold 7 cards plus a trainer.
- **Bot opponents.** Five difficulty tiers. Higher tiers bring rarer sticks, use power-ups and make fewer random moves.
- **Local PvP.** Host or join a game over the LAN and stake 1–3 sticks; the winner takes copies of the loser's stake. Commit-reveal stops either player from cheating.
- **Economy.** Coins come from wins, a 7-day login streak and steps. You spend them on booster packs and the weekly card store.
- **Trainers.** A trainer card levels up as you win and walk, giving HP, stamina, defence or extra deck slots.
- **Cloud backup.** Saves go to the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive and are restored when you sign in on a new device.
- **Tamper resistance.** The local database is encrypted with SQLCipher, and every card row is HMAC-signed with a key in Android Keystore.

## Quick start

1. Open the project in a recent Android Studio. It uses AGP 9.5, Kotlin 2.2, compileSdk 37 and JDK 17+.
2. Optionally create `local.properties` with your Google OAuth client id and admin emails (see [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md)).
3. Run the `app` configuration on a device or emulator.

Debug builds let you **continue offline** without a Google account. Release builds require Google sign-in.
